BBD University — Rules & Regulations

Student conduct guide for academics, discipline and campus safety.

Student-friendly summary based on the rules supplied for this portal. Current official university circulars and programme-specific regulations take precedence.
1. Attendance & classroom rules
  • Attend classes regularly and arrive punctually; aim to reach the lecture hall five minutes before class.
  • Ask the concerned teacher for permission before entering or leaving class mid-session.
  • Maintain classroom discipline and avoid disturbing teaching.
  • Complete assignments, projects, practical records and submissions by deadlines.
  • Attend mentor/counselling sessions when called.
  • Check attendance and exam eligibility under the regulations applicable to your course.
2. ID card & dress code
  • Carry and display your university ID card while on campus, as required by university rules.
  • Follow the prescribed dress code and campus safety instructions.
  • Follow valid instructions from security staff and university authorities.
3. Campus discipline
  • Treat faculty, staff and fellow students respectfully.
  • Do not engage in violence, threats, harassment or misconduct.
  • Do not damage university property, furniture, lab equipment or facilities.
  • Ragging and harassment are prohibited; report incidents to the appropriate university authority.
  • Regularly check official notices, departmental instructions and the university website.
  • Follow university rules regarding canteen use and designated areas during working hours.
4. Mobile phones & technology
  • Follow restrictions on mobile-phone use in classrooms, laboratories and examination halls.
  • The supplied summary notes restrictions on charging phones on campus; confirm the currently applicable instruction with the university.
  • Use computer labs, networks and laboratory equipment only as permitted.
5. Examination rules
  • Cheating, unfair means and impersonation in examinations are prohibited.
  • Misbehaviour with invigilators or examination staff may be treated as serious misconduct.
  • Use only materials permitted by examination instructions.
  • Check official notices for CIA/internal assessment, practical and semester examination dates.
6. Smoking, tobacco & campus cleanliness
  • Follow the university prohibition on smoking, tobacco, pan masala and alcohol on campus.
  • Do not create disturbances through unauthorized slogans or shouting.
  • Do not write on walls or put up unauthorized posters.
  • Use bins and avoid littering or spitting on campus.
  • Switch off lights and fans when leaving a classroom, where appropriate.
7. Vehicles & parking
  • Ride a two-wheeler only with a valid driving licence and use a helmet.
  • Use a seat belt in a car and follow campus traffic/speed rules.
  • Do not exceed the permitted number of riders on a two-wheeler.
  • Park only in designated parking areas.
8. Possible disciplinary action
  • Depending on the violation and applicable university process, action may include warning, monetary fine, suspension, dismissal or withholding of a degree.
  • Consequences depend on the seriousness of the conduct and applicable disciplinary procedure.
9. Central Library & reading areas
  • Follow library entry, ID and bag-storage instructions.
  • Keep quiet and silence devices in reading areas.
  • Check the circulation desk for borrowing limits, renewals and overdue charges.
  • Do not damage books or share licensed digital-resource credentials.
  • Ask library staff for help locating books and journals.
10. Computer, programming & AI labs
  • Use labs only during authorized hours and for permitted academic work.
  • Keep passwords private and sign out of shared computers.
  • Do not install software or change system settings without permission.
  • Unauthorized network scanning and access attempts are prohibited.
  • Do not upload confidential university or personal data to AI tools without authorization.
11. Science & engineering lab safety
  • Read experiment instructions before starting.
  • Use required PPE such as goggles, gloves or lab coats.
  • Handle chemicals and electrical equipment only as instructed.
  • Operate machines only after training and permission.
  • Report spills, injuries, breakage and unsafe equipment promptly.
12. Workshops, fabrication & robotics
  • Use machine tools, 3D printers and soldering stations only with authorization.
  • Use appropriate eye protection and ventilation.
  • Keep people clear of moving robots.
  • Dispose of batteries and electronic waste through designated procedures.
13. Hostel & residential life
  • Confirm current gate timings, leave and visitor procedures with the warden.
  • Keep rooms clean and exits clear.
  • Do not tamper with electrical fittings or fire-safety systems.
  • Report faults, leaks and safety concerns.
  • Follow mess hygiene and shared-space guidance.
14. Sports, transport & parking services
  • Confirm sports and gym access and timings with the responsible office.
  • Verify bus routes, stops, fees and schedules with the transport office.
  • Park only in designated areas.
  • Keep fire lanes and emergency access clear.
15. Wi-Fi, IT support & privacy
  • Use your assigned network account responsibly.
  • Do not disrupt networks or bypass access controls.
  • Do not share another person's grades, records or research without permission.
  • Report phishing, suspicious logins and IT outages to authorized support.
16. Medical, fire & emergencies
  • Move away from immediate danger and alert others if safe.
  • Seek medical assistance promptly for injury or sudden illness.
  • During fire, use a safe exit, avoid lifts and follow evacuation instructions.
  • Use emergency equipment only if trained and safe to do so.
  • Verify emergency contact numbers with the university before relying on them.
17. Grievances & complaints
  • Use the official grievance or anti-ragging mechanism relevant to the issue.
  • For immediate danger, contact campus security or emergency services.
  • Report broken equipment and maintenance concerns through designated channels.
  • Do not publish identity documents or sensitive complaint details publicly.
18. Fees & scholarships
  • Use current fee circulars, scholarship notices and official portals.
  • Check required documents and deadlines with the responsible office.
  • Use authorized payment channels and retain receipts.
  • Scholarship approval depends on scheme rules and verification.
UP Scholarship Portal ↗
19. Placement, internships & career support
  • Follow official placement eligibility and registration instructions.
  • Keep your resume and academic information accurate.
  • Confirm internship approval and documentation with your department where required.
  • Ask the relevant centre about mentoring and project support.
Useful official links: BBDU policy documents · BBDU Library. This student guide does not replace current university rules.

Scholarship Support

UP Scholarship and other government scholarship guidance. Check the current scheme notice before applying.

UP Scholarship Portal

Use the official portal to register or log in, complete the relevant application, and check current notices.

Open UP Scholarship ↗ National Scholarship Portal ↗ Explore government schemes ↗
✅

Eligibility pre-check

  • Check UP domicile/residence requirements for the selected scheme.
  • Select the correct category and scholarship scheme.
  • Verify the family-income limit for that scheme.
  • Confirm that BBDU, your course and your academic year are covered.
  • Check qualifying-exam marks and certificate validity.

Eligibility limits and conditions vary by scheme. The portal's current notice takes precedence.

📂

Scholarship document checklist

Not every scheme requires every document. Confirm the final list on the official portal.

🧭

Application workflow

  1. Read the current scheme notification, eligibility and deadline.
  2. Complete OTR/registration if required by the current scheme.
  3. Choose Fresh or Renewal correctly.
  4. Select the correct category, course, academic year and institution.
  5. Enter details exactly as they appear on official documents.
  6. Review and submit; save the application number and form copy.
  7. Submit a printed copy/documents to the college if required.
  8. Track institute verification, defects, sanction and payment status.
🔍

Before you submit

🛡️

Status, corrections & safety

  • Check application, institute verification, sanction and payment status through official channels.
  • If a defect is raised, follow the exact remark and correction window.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or payment.
  • OTR, e-KYC and bank verification requirements can differ by scheme.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or full banking credentials with unauthorized people.
  • For college-specific verification questions, contact the college scholarship/nodal office.

This is a student-help guide, not a government order. Current eligibility, dates, document requirements and payment instructions must be confirmed on the official portal.
